OpenCode sells access to these models two ways — the Zen pay-as-you-go catalog and the $10/mo Go plan — and OpenRouter is a third-party provider reselling the same models on its own terms.

Where each row comes from

  • (Zen) — OpenCode pay-as-you-go, billed per token at the listed rate.
  • (Go) — included in the $10/mo Go plan; the usage included per period is listed under Go Limits.
  • (Go · Zen) — on the Go plan and on Zen pay-as-you-go at the same price, so one row covers both.
  • (OpenRouter) — OpenRouter's own pricing and context window, which can differ from the same model's Zen/Go twins; OpenRouter adds a 5.5% charge on top of the listed price.
  • A model the two OpenCode plans price differently is listed twice instead — once as (Go), once as (Zen) — each row at its own plan's price.
  • A (New) badge marks a row added to this page within the last month; on narrow screens it is left out of the card header to keep it short.

Reading the table

  • Best Price per Model (the default) lists each model once, on whichever platform charges least for output, plus its free tier where it has one — so a model carried by two or three platforms is compared rather than repeated.
  • The Plan filter shows all rows, newly added (New) models, models available on Zen pay-as-you-go, the Go-plan subset, OpenRouter programming-category models, or free models.
  • Click a column header or its ⇅ button to sort; choosing a new column keeps the previous sort as the tiebreaker, and missing scores (—) sort last.
  • The benchmark columns — AA Coding Index, SWE-bench Pro, Bug Hunt, FrontierCode 2.0, Terminal-Bench 2 & 4 and DeepSWE — each link to their own leaderboard from the header.
  • All prices are USD per 1M tokens. On narrow screens each model collapses to a card: tap it to expand its scores and costs.

What Rank measures

Rank is an objective composite calculated across 7 benchmark suites — Terminal-Bench 4 & 2, DeepSWE, SWE-bench Pro, Bug Hunt, FrontierCode 2.0 and the AA Coding Index — using a log-odds latent-skill model with 2-parameter Mean-Sigma cohort equating and conservative Bayesian lower-confidence-bound (LCB) weighting. A rank resting only on inferred scores carries a trailing “?”.
